# Bird cage concept

`bird-cage.png` is the initial brainstorming board produced with the built-in
image generation tool. It is a reference, not the production bar asset.
Production SVGs and `CageIcon.qml` use original simplified 24px vector geometry.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Preview concept sheet for Faraday, an Omarchy Linux laptop lockdown status-bar widget. Design a beautiful minimal bird inside a domed Faraday cage icon, simple geometric vector-like linework with a solid small bird silhouette on a perch, very few cage bars, generous negative space so the bird is unmistakable at small sizes. Flat charcoal background, crisp strokes, no gradients, no 3D, no decorative illustration. A polished compact horizontal concept board titled 'faraday' in small understated lowercase type. Three equally spaced large glyphs of the SAME cage and bird design, with smaller status-bar-scale repeats beneath each. Left: muted gray cage with visibly open door and bird still perched inside, label 'Off'. Center: pale green fully closed cage with bird inside, label 'Sealed'. Right: warm amber fully closed cage with bird inside and a tiny distinct Wi-Fi arc badge just outside upper-right, label 'Manual Wi-Fi'. Keep the cage CLOSED for Manual Wi-Fi because firewall and lock protection remain on. Shapes as well as colors distinguish modes. Bird silhouette must remain prominent and recognizable, cage no more than three thin vertical bars and bars must not obscure bird. This is icon design exploration, not an application screenshot. No shields, no padlocks, no extra logos.
